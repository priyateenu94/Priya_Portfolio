Profile

Clinical depth, held together by structure.

Priyadharshini K is a clinical psychologist working at the point where individual therapeutic care meets the systems that make care reliable — assessment, formulation, psychotherapy, rehabilitation planning, family work, crisis response, supervision and governance.

Her practice began at Naveen Hospital for Mental Health, conducting case-history interviews and psychoeducation alongside psychiatrists, administering intelligence and personality assessments, and building long-term rehabilitation plans for clients needing continued psychosocial support. She later joined Abhasa Rehab & Wellness as a clinical psychologist, moved into case management for complex and high-risk presentations, and now leads the clinical vertical.

As Head of Clinical & Therapeutic Services she supervises clinical psychologists, psychologists and counsellors, directs treatment quality and crisis intervention, reviews psychometric findings, and has been central to writing and implementing the clinical SOPs and therapeutic service models the organisation runs on.

Clinical approach

  • Informed consent

    Clients understand the diagnosis, the plan and the choices in front of them before work begins.
  • Confidentiality

    Clinical information is protected as a condition of care, not an afterthought.
  • Cultural sensitivity

    Family structure, language and belief are treated as clinical context, not noise.
  • Collaborative goals

    Treatment goals are set with the client and family, and revised as the presentation changes.
  • Continuous evaluation

    Progress is monitored against measurable clinical goals and response to treatment.
  • Trauma-informed care

    Evidence-based psychology delivered through a holistic, person-centred lens.
